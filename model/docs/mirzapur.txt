Mirzapur: The Movie — Everything you need to know about Pankaj Tripathi, Ali Fazal and Shweta Tripathi's crime saga
The upcoming project brings back familiar faces while introducing new twists to the story
By Gaurav Sonavane
6 February 2026
Mirzapur The Movie

After taking the OTT space by storm and becoming one of India’s most talked-about web series, Mirzapur is now making a powerful leap to the big screen with Mirzapur: The Movie. The film expands the gritty universe of power, revenge, and lawlessness, reviving the raw intensity and larger-than-life conflicts that made the show a cultural phenomenon. Fueled by ruthless ambition, explosive action, and emotional drama, Mirzapur: The Movie is expected to delve deeper into the blood-soaked world of Purvanchal. Scroll down to know more about Mirzapur: The Movie—including its release date, cast members, and other key details.
Mirzapur: The Movie — Everything you need to know about Pankaj Tripathi, Ali Fazal and Shweta Tripathi's crime saga

Building on the legacy of the hit series, Mirzapur: The Movie will bring back familiar faces while introducing new twists to the story. Pankaj Tripathi, Ali Fazal, and Shweta Tripathi, among others, will reprise their iconic roles in what is expected to feature higher stakes and unexpected turns. With the shift from episodic storytelling to a cinematic format, the movie aims to deliver a more intense and visually grand experience while remaining true to the dark, brutal spirit of the Mirzapur universe.
What to expect

Given the return of several characters who had died in the series, Mirzapur: The Movie is likely to explore events that occurred before the timeline of the show. The movie may delve into the origins of key characters, tracing how rivalries were formed and how the power structure of Mirzapur initially took shape. By revisiting the past, the movie could offer deeper insights into the rise of crime, ambition, and betrayal in Purvanchal, adding new layers to the story.
Release date

After lead actors Ali Fazal and Shweta Tripathi shared behind-the-scenes moments on social media to celebrate the completion of the final schedule, the makers officially announced the release date. Mirzapur: The Movie is set to hit theatres on September 4, 2026.

Cast of Mirzapur: The Movie

Pankaj Tripathi returns as Akhandanand 'Kaleen Bhaiya' Tripathi, alongside Ali Fazal as Govind 'Guddu' Pandit and Divyenndu as Phoolchand 'Munna Bhaiya' Tripathi. The movie also features Shweta Tripathi reprise her role as Gajgamini 'Golu' Gupta, with Rasika Dugal returning as Beena Tripathi. Familiar faces such as Abhishek Banerjee (Subodh aka Compounder), Shriya Pilgaonkar (Sweety Gupta), Harshita Gaur (Dimpy Pandit), Sheeba Chadha (Vasudha Pandit), Rajesh Tailang (Ramakant Pandit), and Kulbhushan Kharbanda as Satyanand Tripathi, also known as Bauji, are part of the ensemble.

In addition to the returning cast, Jitendra Kumar—of Panchayat fame—is reportedly set to play Bablu Pandit, stepping into the role after Vikrant Massey chose not to return. The movie also introduces new faces to the Mirzapur universe, including Ravi Kishan, Mohit Malik, and Sonal Chauhan.
